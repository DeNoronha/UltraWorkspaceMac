Reporting a problem

What helps me help you.

  1. Versions.Deckhand's version (Deckhand → About Deckhand), your macOS version, and the bar's launcher version if you know it.
  2. What you did and what happened.The steps, what you expected, and the exact message Deckhand or the bar showed.
  3. A screenshot.One of Deckhand, or a photo of the bar, says more than a description.
  4. Leave out secrets.Never send passwords, API keys, tokens or your bar's serial number; I will never ask for them.
